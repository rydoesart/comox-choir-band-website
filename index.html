What this festival is about

The Comox Valley Band & Choir Festival brings students and educators together for a day of music-making, learning, and celebration. Featuring performances, workshops, and guidance from professional musicians, the festival creates a supportive space for young artists to grow, connect, and share their passion for music with the community.
